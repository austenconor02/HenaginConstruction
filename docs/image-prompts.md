# Marketing imagery and generation prompts

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, not the API/CLI fallback. These are illustrative marketing scenes, not photographs of Henagin staff or completed customer work. All three outputs were visually inspected. The supplied original logo is used separately in the website, without recoloring, redrawing, or AI regeneration. This avoids presenting an approximate generated logo on clothing as the original.

Optimized final assets:

- `public/images/marketing/remodeling.webp` and `remodeling-small.webp`
- `public/images/marketing/painting.webp` and `painting-small.webp`
- `public/images/marketing/exterior.webp` and `exterior-small.webp`

The 1440px/640px sources are responsive WebPs at quality 82. Original generated PNGs are preserved in `assets/source/`. The current company logo is the separately supplied no-phone PNG, retained at `assets/source/logo-no-phone-original.png` and converted to `public/images/logo-no-phone.webp`. Its exterior padding is cropped and near-black exterior matched to pure black; artwork colors and proportions are preserved. The earlier logo remains archived at `assets/source/logo-original.jpg`. The honeycomb texture is deterministic geometry in `scripts/prepare-assets.mjs`, not generated by AI.

## Remodeling — exact prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide landscape hero photograph for Henagin Home Improvement, a Dallas–Fort Worth residential construction website. Create a premium photorealistic editorial marketing illustration of a home remodeling scene: a beautifully designed warm contemporary kitchen, off-white quartz island, warm oak cabinetry, charcoal accents, brushed brass fixtures, natural Texas afternoon light. One adult professional carpenter in charcoal work shirt with subtle gold piping and dark work trousers is carefully measuring a cabinet with a tape measure. Appropriate clear safety glasses, realistic hands and tools. Interior should feel achievable, welcoming, lived-in and refined, not an extravagant mansion. Wide landscape composition, 3:2, kitchen prominent, worker to right, quietly cinematic light and material detail. No readable text, no logos on clothing, no watermark, no before-after. This is illustrative marketing imagery, not a real customer project. Branding will be supplied separately in the webpage using the exact original company logo.
